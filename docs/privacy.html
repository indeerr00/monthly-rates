Privacy policy

Monthly collects no personal information. This page explains exactly what that means.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

The short version

Everything you type into Monthly stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We cannot see your numbers, because they are never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Home prices, down payments, rates, terms, saved scenarios and app settings are stored on the iPhone itself, in the app's own storage. They are not transmitted to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Monthly has no sign-in and no user accounts, so nothing you enter is ever associated with your identity.

What leaves your device

Mortgage rates
The app downloads a public rates file published at github.com/indeerr00/monthly-rates. The request carries no personal data and nothing about your calculations — it fetches the same file for every user. As with any web request, the server hosting the file (GitHub) receives your device's IP address and standard request headers.
Feedback you choose to send
If you tap "Send feedback", your own mail app opens with a message pre-filled with the app version, your iOS version and your device model. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and you can edit or delete any of it first. We receive only what you send, and use it only to reply and to fix the problem.
Crash reports
If you have enabled sharing analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash diagnostics. This is Apple's mechanism, not ours, and you control it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. These reports contain no mortgage data.

What we do not do

No analytics or usage tracking. No advertising or ad identifiers. No third-party SDKs that collect data. No selling or sharing of information, because there is none to sell. No location, contacts, photos, or health data — the app never requests access to them.

Children

Monthly is a general-purpose financial calculator and is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Rates data and attribution

Rate figures shown in the app come from the Optimal Blue Mortgage Market Indices (OBMMIC30YF, OBMMIC15YF), retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. They are national indices provided for information only. They are not an offer of credit, and your own rate will depend on your credit, the property, points and your lender.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear on this page with a new date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: monthlyapp.support@gmail.com.

Monthly is an estimator, not a lender, and nothing it shows is a loan offer or financial advice.

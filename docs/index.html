Common questions

What is P&I?

Principal and interest — the part of your payment that goes to the loan itself. Principal pays down what you borrowed; interest is what the lender charges. Taxes, insurance, PMI and HOA are collected with the payment but passed on to others.

Why did PMI appear?

Private mortgage insurance is usually required on conventional loans with less than 20% down. Monthly estimates it at your PMI rate (0.55%/yr of the loan by default) and drops it automatically at 20% or more down.

Where do the rates come from?

The Optimal Blue Mortgage Market Indices, retrieved from FRED at the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. They are computed from rates borrowers actually locked and update every weekday morning. Your own quote depends on credit, points and lender.

Why doesn't the app match the rate I saw in the news?

News coverage usually quotes Freddie Mac's weekly survey, which asks lenders what they're offering. Monthly shows the daily locked-rate index instead. The two track each other but rarely match exactly, and the daily one moves sooner.

What's the difference between fixed and interest-only?

A fixed loan pays the balance down to zero over the term. An interest-only loan pays only the interest, so the payment is lower but the full balance is still owed at the end of the term. Monthly shows that balance rather than hiding it.

Is the estimate exact?

Close, but it is not a quote. Property tax and insurance vary by county and by home, and lenders round differently. Open the taxes & insurance section and enter your real numbers for a closer figure.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Saved scenarios and settings never leave the device. See the privacy policy.

Does it cost anything?

No. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.

Monthly is an estimator, not a lender, and nothing it shows is a loan offer or financial advice.
